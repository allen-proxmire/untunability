## Item by item

**Status key:**
- **Supplied:** ED's structure forces it, with nothing fitted.
- **Relocated:** physics treats it as a boundary condition or an interpretation; ED makes it part of the law.
- **Reinterpreted:** ED says it in its own language, consistent, not derived.
- **Inherited:** taken from physics as is.
- **Added:** ED needs it, and physics doesn't.
- **Conflict:** a decided ED meaning runs into an observation.
- **Not reached.**
