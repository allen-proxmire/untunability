## What this audit says

1. **We have been testing the borrowed half of ED.** Budgets and conditions are ED's, but the substrate, the moves and the layer are borrowed, and the parts you describe as the heart of ED — clocks and rates — are absent.
2. **"Space collapses" may be an artefact of the borrowed substrate.** A network of events has no dimension; you measure one on it. We forced ED's network into a sheet of triangles and then measured that sheet.
3. **Synced Now, the one result that got three dimensions out of ED's own content, was never carried into these models.** It sits in attempt 6, unconnected to everything since.
4. **The strongest current claim survives this audit:** ED's budgets fix the three numbers CDT tunes. That's about conservation and counting, and it doesn't depend on clocks. But it is a claim about the borrowed machinery, not about ED's own picture.

## What follows (for the discussion you asked for)

The road you floated — **space is not assumed** — would be: ED as a network of events with rates, no sheet, no triangles, dimension only as a reading. The audit says that road is not a detour: **it's the part of ED we have never tested.**

Three questions to settle when we take it up, in your words as far as possible:
- **What is a tick without a sheet?** An event passes on to a neighbourhood — but a neighbourhood of what, if not of space?
- **What do clocks do?** In attempt 6 rates agreeing gave three dimensions. Is that the mechanism that should make space, rather than any geometric rule?
- **What would count as space appearing?** If dimension is only a reading, we need to say beforehand what reading on a network would mean "3D space", and what would mean "no space".
