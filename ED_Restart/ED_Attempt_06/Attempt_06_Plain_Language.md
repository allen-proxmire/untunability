## 1. How waves travel on a random pattern (road W, notes 2–3)

**Long waves:** on a random pattern with no preferred directions they see a smooth medium, the same every way (C3).

**Short waves:** for spreading rules, every short wave is slower than light on any pattern (C4). ED's own wave rule is a quantum walk, which that argument doesn't cover (C8).

**The leak:** the worry that slow short waves let atoms feel Lorentz violation depends on an idealized detector that never recoils. In ED it becomes two better-posed questions (C11).

**Stuck states:** with no stored directions, amplitude circling loops never moves. At least 1 − 2/(average neighbours) of all states are stuck (C12, C13).

**Verdict** (C15): road W closed with a wall. *(Reframed in section 4.)*

## 2. Growing the pattern (road G, notes 4–7)

- **Adding never stretches:** new points and links only add routes, so adding alone can't expand anything (C17).
- **Random growth loses dimension,** so growth needs something it favours (C17).
- **Clocks want to sync, and commitment wants the fewest directions.** Rates can only match above two dimensions: a patch's edge must outgrow the random surplus of its rates. So three is the fewest whole-number dimension where clocks can sync (C29, C30). **A reason for three from ED's meanings, consistent, not derived** (C33).
- **But the step-by-step process heads to just above two dimensions,** and nothing found made the pattern smooth (C36, C40).

## 3. What makes a pattern smooth (note 9)

- **Mathematics has a recipe:** curvature bounded below, a finite ceiling on dimension, and quadratic energy give a pattern that is smooth almost everywhere, with one whole-number dimension. Fractals fail it (C42, C43).
- **ED's meanings offer a reading for each ingredient:** sync as contraction, no infinities, quadratic energy. With the sync floor, three comes out of conditions rather than assumed smoothness (C44).
- **The price is many short loops** (C45).
- **Verdict** (C47): consistent, not derived; the wall is reframed as conditions a growth rule must keep.

## 4. Loops: stuck states and smoothness (note 10)

- **Every wave state splits exactly** into a moving sector (built from values at points, run by the pattern's own random walk) and a stuck sector (C49, checked with no misses, C50).
- **Ordinary matter only touches the moving sector,** so stuck states are **dark** and loops don't trap light (C49).
- **Light and smoothness run on the same operator:** the pattern's random walk.
- **Verdict** (C54): the stuck-state conflict is removed, conditionally, and reframed as a large dark sector.

## 6. The missing ingredient: time order (notes 15–16)

- **Both models grew space without time.** Links had no direction, and space could branch freely.
- **In quantum gravity research,** that freedom gives fractal and crumpled geometries. **Causality is what fixes it:** CDT grows a smooth four-dimensional world because space isn't allowed to split or merge as time goes on (C82).
- **ED already has the ingredients:** a strict time order, and the grain as a count. That's causal set theory's "order plus number equals geometry" (C83).
- **But no known rule does everything ED needs:** finite neighbours, no branching, no built-in dimension, and a smooth pattern that stays (C87, C88).
- **2D CDT shows exactly** that causality gives smoothness once the dimension of space's slices is given. So ED needs two halves joined:
  - **causal growth with no branching,** for smoothness;
  - **sync and commitment picking three,** for the dimension.
- **So M2's failure is the expected result for space-without-time.** It doesn't count against ED's picture with time order.

## Words used here

- **Point / locus:** one place in ED's pattern.
- **Link / relation:** a connection between two points.
- **Ball-cut:** the number of links crossing the surface of a ball; it grows like area in a space-like pattern.
- **Stuck state:** a wave state that flips back and forth on a loop and never moves.
- **Moving sector:** wave states built from values at points. Light lives here.
- **Dark:** never excited by, and never emitting to, ordinary matter.
- **Curvature floor:** a limit on how fast neighbourhoods may spread apart.
- **Mass dimension, walk dimension:** ways of reading dimension. Counting points within a distance, and how far a random walk spreads. Smooth patterns have walk dimension about 2.
- **Small world:** everything within a few steps of everything; no finite dimension.
- **Slice:** a set of events none of which comes before another. Space at a moment.
- **Smooth:** looks like ordinary space at large scale: one whole-number dimension, no fractal roughness.
