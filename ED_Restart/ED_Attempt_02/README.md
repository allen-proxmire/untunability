## Status: concluded

**Read first:** [Attempt_02_Plain_Language.md](Attempt_02_Plain_Language.md), the plain-language write-up.

**What stands:**
- **ED's draw probabilities are forced** by its structure, not chosen (C11).
- **Unexplained inputs of physics cluster** in the relation and boundary classes (C27).
- **ED's original coin-and-shift rule needs no standing phase** (C42).
- **The fair (Fourier) coin is the one three-channel coin that hosts a chance-chosen hand,** robust across ring size, spacing, draw strength and feedback form (C53, C58, C59, C62–C64).
- **Conserving the motion passed into committed matter caps ED's own feedback,** and giving that motion back doesn't lift the cap (C73–C78).

**What closed, each under an exit rule confirmed before its run:**
- **The discrete handedness line**, as "hosted, not made" (RD12, C74).
- **The moving-matter line** (RD15, C77). With it, the handedness question rests.

**What's open:**
- **Which coin is ED's** (D7).
- **The source of specificity** (note 1). This continues in `../ED_Attempt_03`.
