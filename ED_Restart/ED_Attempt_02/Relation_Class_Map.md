## What's been tried

| structure | what it explains | what it doesn't | status | ledger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Texture zeros** (Weinberg 1977; Fritzsch 1977–78) | Setting some mass-matrix entries to zero makes mixing follow from mass ratios. **This is where GST comes from:** a zero in the right place gives θC = √(m_d/m_s) | Why those zeros; most three-family textures are under pressure from data | A family of models | C31 |
| **Froggatt–Nielsen** (1979) | Hierarchies as powers of one small number set by a broken symmetry's charges; mixing angles ~ √(mass ratios) in order of magnitude | Exact values: every entry carries an unknown order-one coefficient | Widely used | C30 |
| **Discrete flavour symmetries** (A4, S4, …; Altarelli–Feruglio) | Large lepton mixing patterns, e.g. tri-bimaximal | **Exact tri-bimaximal was ruled out** when θ13 was measured nonzero (Daya Bay, RENO, 2012); modified versions survive with more ingredients | Alive, but many choices | C32 |
| **Modular flavour symmetry** (Feruglio 2017 onward) | Couplings become functions of **one field value** (the modulus τ). Hierarchies arise when τ sits **near a special fixed point** (τ = i or ω = e^{2πi/3}), with no extra fields. With spontaneously broken CP, the **strong-CP angle vanishes while the CKM phase stays large** | Koide and complementarity exactly; order-one coefficients remain; many possible models | Very active | C33–C35 |
| **Sumino's family gauge symmetry** (2009) | **Koide exactly:** a new family force cancels the correction that would otherwise spoil it | Anything beyond charged leptons; needs new heavy gauge bosons | A specific model | C36 |
| **Strong-CP solutions** | The axion (Peccei–Quinn) removes θ̄ | **A massless up quark is ruled out** (lattice, 2020); Nelson–Barr needs its own fine-tuning | The axion is alive and searched for | C37 |
| **Anarchy** (Hall, Murayama, Weiner 2000) | Neutrino mixings look like **random** numbers | Any pattern: its answer is that there may be no structure | A serious null option | C38 |

## The reading (C39)

**Modular flavour symmetry is the closest thing to "one principle, several inputs".** It reaches hierarchies, mixings and the CP phases, including θ̄ = 0, through a single field value.

**Where its specificity comes from matters most.** The special numbers come from **the state sitting at or near a fixed point of a symmetry**, not from minimizing a cost. In Allen's terms (D1), that's a **boundary** fact (where the state sits) fixing **relation** numbers (how particles couple). It is exactly the shape note 1 described: a source of specificity that isn't dynamics.

**It isn't number-free.** Order-one coefficients remain, and there are many models. So it's a direction, not an answer.

**A resemblance worth a flag (look-elsewhere applies):** 120° keeps appearing.
- **Koide:** in Brannen's form, the three lepton masses sit at points 120° apart (a Z₃ structure).
- **Modular symmetry:** one of its special fixed points is ω = e^{2πi/3}, a 120° phase.
- **ED's coin:** it treats its three channels (Internal, Left, Right) all alike. That's a full permutation symmetry of three objects, the same group (S₃) that Koide-type flavour models use.

Each could be coincidence.

## What this suggests for ED (a question, not a claim)

**Why attempt 1's three tries failed, in this light.** They all tried to fix the standing phase by **minimizing a cost** (coherence), and costs kept landing on 0 or π.

**The literature suggests a different mechanism:** a **fixed point of a symmetry acting on a phase-like parameter**. The concrete ED question:

> Does ED's three-channel structure carry a symmetry (for example the cyclic Z₃ inside the coin's S₃) whose fixed points put the lane-loop phase at ±120°? Those values are neither 0 nor π, so they allow a hand. The size would be fixed by the symmetry and the sign chosen by the state, which is exactly the "specific magnitude, chance-chosen hand" that ED's handedness needed.

**If yes,** the phase would be forced rather than tuned. The machinery to test the hand at a fixed phase already exists (A1-ledger C288, C293).

**If no,** that's a clean negative.
