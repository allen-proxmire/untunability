## The two results attempt 11 leaves standing

### 1. The strongest claim the project has (A11 C5, C8)

CDT is the standard, well-developed approach to quantum spacetime. It has **three totals that have to be tuned by hand**. ED doesn't tune them: its conserved budgets fix two, and conserving forward links fixes the third. **ED lands on one point of CDT's map with no dials touched** — and in 2+1 that point sits **inside the phase where space doesn't collapse**.

- Exact algebra, not a simulation.
- **A genuine reduction under the census guard:** fewer free numbers, none added.
- **Its limit, stated plainly:** it's a claim about the borrowed machinery. It says that if you accept a CDT-style description, ED's budgets pick the point instead of tuning it. It does not say ED's own picture makes space.

### 2. The finding of record: a shape is carried, not chosen (A10 C33, A11 C23)

Two routes that share almost nothing:

- **Attempt 10** — borrowed triangles, counting whole histories. Every history stayed at its start: flat stayed flat, crowded stayed crowded, long loops and short, with and without whole-history moves.
- **Attempt 11** — ED's own clocks, rates and relations, no geometry anywhere. A ring stays a ring, a flat grid stays flat, a 3D grid stays 3D, a web stays a web.

**Same answer from opposite directions.** That's why it's the thing attempt 12 is built on.

## What attempt 11 did **not** establish

- **Three dimensions from ED's rules.** Sync rules out one and two — that part is real — but "fewest relations that still hold" then selects a **small world**, not a 3D lattice. Small worlds hold clocks together *more cheaply* than 3D grids do.
- **Where a new event goes.** This is the thing that decides the shape, and ED says nothing about it.
- **Space derived.** ED keeps space. It does not, so far, make it.

## The opening road: P, persistence

**The question:** *does ED keep a shape, and keep it sync-able — and is that a result or a restatement?*

**Why this road:** it's the one statement two independent attempts both landed on, and it has never been tested as a claim in its own right. Everything so far found persistence as a *side effect* while looking for something else.

**The cost, named up front:** if persistence becomes ED's claim, **the shape becomes a fourth supplied input**, alongside the three you already supply. **ED's input list grows.** Under the census guard that is **not** a reduction, and this attempt will not write it up as one. What it can honestly be is an *explanation* — of why space, once there, stays and stays sync-able.

**The first test, and it's one road N never ran:** spent events, generation-wise growth, and the sync filter **all running at once**. Each was probed alone; the combination is the only configuration where all of ED's own parts are in at the same time. It's also the sharpest test of persistence, so it serves both purposes.
