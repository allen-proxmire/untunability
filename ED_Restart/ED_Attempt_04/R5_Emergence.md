# Road R5, part 4: emergence (on paper)

*ED_Attempt_04, note 10. 2026-09-15 (RD14, D19). Ledger: C58–C60. Literature and reasoning; nothing computed. **Meaning questions E-Q1–E-Q4 have least-structure defaults for Allen to accept, change or veto; the exit rule is a draft for Allen to confirm.***

## The question

**Where R5 stands:**
- **Allen's ball:** everything related to everything, identically. No distance, no centre (note 6).
- **The first draw is the first difference** (A1 C261).
- **Time is the order of relations; space is their pattern** (D11).
- **Only in three dimensions do uncuttable knots give lasting kinds of particle** (note 9).

**What note 9 left open:** it says a world of lasting particles must be three-dimensional. It doesn't say how the pattern of relations *becomes* three-dimensional, or why a fourth direction never shows up. In Allen's words, "4 is never considered" (D15).

**So R5 emergence asks:** when relations grow out of the ball, does anything in ED make the pattern three-dimensional, with nothing put in by hand?

## What "a direction" means when space is only relations

A pattern of relations has no axes built in. Its dimension has to be **read off the pattern**. The known ways:

| reading | how it works | used by |
|---|---|---|
| **Counting outward** | Count how many loci are within n steps. In d dimensions that grows like n^d | Causal dynamical triangulations; the random-graph paper below |
| **Spreading** | How fast a random walk spreads out, or how rarely it returns | Causal dynamical triangulations; causal sets |
| **Order** | In a set of events ordered in time, the share of pairs that are related | Causal sets (Myrheim–Meyer) |
| **Knots** | The dimension in which loops of relations keep their kind | ED only (note 9) |

**Counting outward uses only counts,** which is all ED allows as real (A1 RD15). The knot reading is ED's own. But using it as the *definition* would make "knots pick three" true by definition, so it's kept as a check, not the definition (E-Q2).

## What's known (C58)

| program | how the dimension comes out | lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **Quantum graphity** (Konopka, Markopoulou, Smolin, Severini) | The rules include a term that prefers each point to have a set number of neighbours, **chosen in advance**. The low-dimensional lattice follows from that choice. Simple versions have many ground states that aren't lattices at all (Spector and Schwartz 2018) | **The dimension is put in** through the preferred number of neighbours |
| **Causal dynamical triangulations** (Ambjørn, Jurkiewicz, Loll) | Space-time is built from **four-dimensional building blocks** with a time order. Four dimensions appear at large scales, in one phase only. Without the time order, the result is either crumpled (effectively infinite dimension) or a branching tree (dimension 2) | **The building blocks' dimension is put in**, and an order in time is needed to avoid nonsense |
| **Causal sets** (Sorkin and others) | The typical random order is a flat three-layer stack, nothing like space (the "entropy problem"). The simplest growth rule, one element at a time, avoids that. But it gives repeated bounces with dimension about 1 | **Growth by order alone doesn't pick 3 or 4** |
| **Thinning a complete graph** (Kleftogiannis and Amanatidis, preprint) | Start with every point linked to every other, and remove one link per tick. The graph's size grows fast, then stops growing just before it falls apart into pieces. **At that point, counting outward gives dimension about 3** and the space is nearly flat. At small scales it's about 2. The dimension changes as links are removed | **Closest to Allen's ball.** But 3 is read at one moment, the edge of falling apart, not where the process rests. One preprint |
| **Knotty inflation; string gas** (C54) | Topology selects three large dimensions out of more that already exist | **Selection, with the extra dimensions assumed present** |

## The pattern (C59)

**Every known route to a dimension does one of three things:**
1. **puts the dimension in:** graphity's neighbour count, the triangulations' building blocks;
2. **reads it at one moment of a process:** the thinning graph, just before it falls apart;
3. **gets something that isn't space-like:** causal-set orders and growth, triangulations without a time order.

**None grows three dimensions as a resting state with nothing put in.** That's the bar for ED. Meeting it would be new; missing it is the expected, honest outcome, since this has resisted decades of work.

**A tension inside ED.** The thinning route is the closest match to Allen's ball, and it works by **removing** relations. But in ED a commitment can't be undone (A1) or cut (D14). **If the ball's relations are commitments, the ball can never thin out, and space could never form from it.** So ED has to say what the ball's relations are (E-Q1).

## Three ways emergence could go in ED (C60)

| | route | what it needs | known problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E-A** | **Thinning:** the ball's identical relations switch off, and space is what's left | The ball's relations are **not** commitments (E-Q1) | In the only worked example, 3 appears at one moment, not as a resting state |
| **E-B** | **Growing:** each draw adds commitments, and space is the pattern of commitments | Commitments made by draws, never removed (E-Q3) | Growth by order alone doesn't give space (causal sets). ED would need more than order |
| **E-C** | **Selection by lasting records:** a draw fixes a record (A1 RD50). A record has to be a lasting difference, and lasting kinds exist only where the pattern is three-dimensional (note 9). **So records, and with them commitments building on commitments, pile up only where the pattern is 3D. A fourth direction never carries a record.** That's one reading of "4 is never considered" | D14, D16, A1 RD50, plus E-Q4's answer | It selects three-dimensional patches; it doesn't grow them. It's circular if dimension is defined by knots. And particles annihilate (C55), so "lasting" needs care |

**E-C is the one that's ED's own.** It links three things ED already has:
- **draws make records;**
- **records must last;**
- **only knots in three dimensions last.**

Neither knotty inflation nor string gas cosmology has records. **It's a candidate, not a result.**

## Meaning questions (Allen decides)

| | question | proposed default (least structure) | why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E-Q1** | Are the ball's "everything related identically" relations commitments? | **No.** They're sameness and carry no information ("one is none", A1 D16). Commitments are only the relations draws make | If they were commitments they could never switch off (D14), and the ball could never become space |
| **E-Q2** | How is dimension read from the pattern? | **By counting outward:** how many loci lie within n steps, for large n | Uses only counts (A1 RD15). The knot reading stays a check, so "knots pick three" can't be true by definition |
| **E-Q3** | Once a commitment is made, does it ever stop? | **No** (A1; D14) | Then commitments can only add structure. Thinning, if it happens, applies only to the ball's sameness relations |
| **E-Q4** | Does "4 is never considered" mean a fourth direction never forms, or that it forms but never holds a lasting record? | **Never holds a lasting record** (E-C) | "Never forms" would need a new rule. "No lasting record" uses meanings ED already has |

## Pokes

- **Nothing known meets the bar.** ED hasn't shown it can do better. Causal dynamical triangulations took decades to reach four dimensions, and it puts the building blocks in by hand.
- **E-C selects, it doesn't grow.** That's the same limit as knotty inflation, minus the hidden extra dimensions.
- **The thinning result is one preprint.** It isn't reproduced here, and its 3 sits at the edge of falling apart.
- **About 2 at small scales shows up in several programs:** triangulations, causal sets and the thinning graph. It's logged as a hint, not tested.
- **Annihilation (C55) now matters directly.** E-C needs lasting kinds, and particles annihilate.
