## The thing to chase

Eight seeds said: **a line is carried perfectly (16 of 16, gap 3.4–3.9), a cubic grid is not (1 of 8, gap 1.5–1.7), a random web never gains anything.** I recorded that without an explanation. Here is the one that has to be ruled out first.

**It may not be about ED at all.**

The reading counts a group of lowest modes and needs a clear gap above it. **How clear that gap is differs by shape, before anything is done to the pattern.** On a clean ring the next modes sit about four times higher. On a clean cubic torus they sit about twice as high. **So three dimensions has an intrinsically weaker signature than one, and any blurring will destroy it sooner** — whatever the blurring is, and whoever does it.

If that is the whole story, then ED's growth isn't singling out three dimensions for punishment. It is adding ordinary randomness, and three simply has less margin than one.

**That would make the asymmetry an instrument fact, not an ED fact — and I would rather find that out than write it up as a discovery.**

## The test

**Three steps, and the third is the one that decides.**

| | step |
|---|---|
| **A1** | Measure the gap on **clean** references — ring, flat torus, cubic torus — at matched sizes and thicknesses. This is the margin each shape starts with |
| **A2** | Blur them with **generic noise**: rewire a fraction of relations at random, and find the fraction at which each shape stops giving a definite reading. This is how much blurring each signature can take |
| **A3** | **The decider:** measure how much blurring ED's growth actually introduces, and ask whether the damage it does to a cubic torus is **more** than generic noise of that size would do |

## Expected results, fixed before any code

## What each outcome means

- **A3 holds:** the asymmetry is an instrument fact. ED is not hostile to three dimensions; three dimensions is simply harder to read once anything is randomised. **C11's striking sentence — that ED's growth is hardest on exactly the shape the project wants — gets withdrawn**, and that is the right outcome for it.
- **A3 fails — ED damages three dimensions more than equivalent noise:** then ED's growth really is doing something specific to higher-dimensional structure, and that is a genuine finding about ED, worth a road of its own.
