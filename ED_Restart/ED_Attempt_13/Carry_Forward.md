## What was corrected, and is **not** carried

**"A shape is carried, not chosen."** Attempts 10 and 11 both landed on it, and attempt 12 showed it was measured with ED's sync requirement switched off. With it on, shape is erased. The A10–A11 agreement stands as a statement about counting and inheritance without sync — not about ED.

## Meanings settled in attempt 12 and carried

- **D4:** a relation can dissolve — commitment is passed on, not held.
- **D5:** sync is local; **there is no shared now.** Rates matching does not put things in one moment, and a "now" would imply a shared space.
- **D6:** a relation persists while it is part of a commitment. Taken with no new number: one that has ever carried something persists; one that never has, lapses.

## What is not established

Three dimensions from ED. Space derived rather than supplied. Where a first shape comes from. **Inputs supplied: three, plus the starting shape in every model so far. Nothing derived.**

## The opening road: E, the end state

**Every test so far asked the same question — "is it three-dimensional space?" — got "no", and moved on.** Nobody has looked at what ED's rules actually make.

**And it is a definite object.** Reached from a line, a lattice and a random web alike. Sparse — about 4 relations per event. Spread out — distances that grow rather than collapse. Holding its rates patch by patch, with no shared now. Chosen by nothing.

**Road E asks what it is**, using the same calibrated readings and whatever else the object calls for: how distances grow, what the neighbourhood of an event looks like, whether it has structure at some scale even if not a dimension, what its patches look like against their edges, and whether the thing is the same object at every size or only looks that way at the sizes we've run.

**Why this road and not another:** it needs no new meanings from Allen, the object is already in the run data, and it is the only question in the project right now that nobody has asked. It may also be the honest form of the project's result — *this is what ED's rules make* — rather than a further attempt to find something we've decided in advance we want.
