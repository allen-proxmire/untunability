## What ED has, honestly

**Standing:**
- **The budgets fix what CDT tunes.** Exact algebra, nothing tuned, no new free numbers, landing inside the phase where space doesn't collapse. **The only genuine reduction the project has**, and — as attempt 13's reading of the January paper showed — exactly the kind of claim ED's own ontology says it makes: a constraint on what is available, not a prediction of a structure.
- **The sync floor, measured.** Below three dimensions clocks cannot hold together at large size; at three and above they can. Argued in attempt 6, measured in attempt 11 on four known shapes.
- **Nothing creates a dimension.** Sixteen runs, ED's own instrument, ED's own regime.
- **A line is carried perfectly, three is not.** ED's growth is hardest on exactly the shape the project wants — new, unexplained, and not invented after the fact.
- **A fragmentation threshold** near 5 relations per event.
- **ED never collapses** in the 2+1 runs.

**Not established, after thirteen attempts:** three dimensions from ED; space produced rather than supplied; where a first shape comes from. **Inputs supplied: three, plus a starting shape in every model. Nothing derived.**

## What the papers say about that, and it matters

The January paper: *"ED does not predict every structure in the universe. It explains why structure is possible... conditions of possibility, not the full catalogue of outcomes."*

Paper 11, one month later: *"The goal is not to reinterpret physical law. The goal is to derive it."*

**Thirteen attempts have graded ED against the second sentence while the evidence keeps landing on the first.** That is not a failure of the work; it is a question about which claim ED is making, and it is Allen's to answer.

## What was learned about how to do this

Five times a model turned out to be missing something ED had already written down — clocks, no shared now, dissolution and commitment, gradients, thickness — and **three of those were caught by Allen from the meaning alone, with no numbers**. The instruments were wrong twice (a pass rule that charged the reading's own drift to the pattern; ball growth disagreeing with ED's definition by more than a dimension). A result was withdrawn on its own check within the hour (the 6.699 match), and another within a day (the thick-regime positive).

**The pattern worth keeping: fix the rule before the run, and build the run that can destroy the result.** Every claim in this project that survived did so because something was written down that could have killed it.
