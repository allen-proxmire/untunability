# The draw rebuilt: results

*Run 2, 2026-09-14 (`results_draw_run2.txt`; run 1 had a test-harness error in D5 and D6, recorded in Spec.md). Spec and predictions frozen before running: [Spec.md](Spec.md). Ledger: RD50, C291, C292, G53.*

## What was built

- **The environment has a state.** Fragments of committed matter, each unmarked or marked.
- **A meeting leaves a mark.** Wherever the system passes certain loci, a fragment turns toward "marked". The meeting is reversible and never draws by itself.
- **A draw fixes only the record.** It fixes which way the fragments say the system went, not a locus and lane. Everything inside that record stays coherent.
- **When a draw happens (the working stand-in).** Once 3 fragments hold near-perfect copies of the same distinction. The real meaning (RD50) is that part of the mark is out of reach of every future path; the 3 is a modelling choice.

## What it means (C292)

**The draw is no longer loose on three of the six points (C286).** It now has:
- a defined trigger (a stand-in for the horizon meaning);
- a defined content (only the record);
- a tracked mark (the environment's state).

**It reproduces the known single-mark, partial-mark and eraser results, and it doesn't signal.** That's a requirement met, not evidence for ED.

**Still open.** The probabilities are assumed (point 4). Whether a draw uses budget is unspecified (point 6). And the stand-in is wrong about erasing many copies (D8b), so it must not be used for claims about reversibility.

**For handedness.** Version 2b's "draws carry motion off" now has a clear place: it's part of what a meeting with committed matter does (the meeting's interaction). The draw itself only fixes the record. Re-checking the tuned hand on this draw means building meetings at committed loci that both mark the lane and pass motion into committed matter (G53).
