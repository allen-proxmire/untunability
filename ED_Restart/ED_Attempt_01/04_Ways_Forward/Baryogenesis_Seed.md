# Matter over antimatter: Allen's seed crystal (D15)

*2026-09-14. Ledger: D15, C248, C251–C258, G46.*

## 1. The idea, in Allen's words

> "like a seed for a crystal decides how the crystal grows... the first difference was the pattern that had to be accepted, and that pattern was matter over antimatter and the handedness. and in some sense, the commitment was too saturated to form other patterns."

It isn't new to Allen's work. ED-I-11 and Paper_ED_Baryogenesis (ED Generative) say the same thing: one template under saturation, seeded by the first arrival (C257).

## 2. What physics says any explanation needs

**Sakharov's three conditions (C252).** To get more matter than antimatter from an even start you need:
1. **Something that changes the number of baryons.**
2. **Matter and antimatter treated differently** (C and CP violation).
3. **A departure from equilibrium**, so the process doesn't simply undo itself.

**How big (C253).** One extra quark per roughly a billion quark–antiquark pairs. Nearly all antimatter did form and annihilate. What's left is the tiny excess.

**Two traps for any spontaneous choice.**
- **Domains (C255):** separate matter and antimatter regions are ruled out within the visible universe.
- **Walls (C254):** where regions chose differently, walls form, and they cause trouble unless the choice was made before or during inflation.

## 3. How the seed idea measures up

| need | seed idea | status |
|---|---|---|
| Departure from equilibrium (3) | ED is irreversible by construction (P11) | **Fits** |
| One hand chosen, no built-in preference | Spontaneous handedness (RD41); toy shows the seed's direction wins (C248, C251) | **Fits, in a toy** |
| Chosen once for everything we see | "Too saturated to form other patterns" would block other regions from choosing differently | **Plausible, untested:** the toy can't form domains yet |
| Matter treated differently from antimatter (2) | A chosen hand *is* that difference once it exists | **Fits in principle**, if a hand couples to matter vs antimatter |
| Changes baryon number (1) | Nothing in ED yet: no baryons, no baryon number | **Missing** |
| About one in a billion | The seed must tilt the outcome slightly, not wipe out antimatter | **Missing**; crystals give 99.7% one hand (C256), a different regime |

**Where the old write-up goes wrong (C257).**
- "Not CP-driven": treating matter and antimatter differently *is* CP violation by definition, so this is a relabel.
- It has antimatter unable to persist, while in fact almost all of it formed and then annihilated.

## 4. Published homes (so it isn't new there)

- **Frank 1953:** a hand that copies itself, with the hands suppressing each other, drives chemistry to one hand (C256).
- **Kondepudi 1990:** stirred sodium chlorate gives 99.7% crystals of one hand, chosen at random (C256).
- **In particle physics**, spontaneous parity breaking is the left–right symmetric models (C240).
