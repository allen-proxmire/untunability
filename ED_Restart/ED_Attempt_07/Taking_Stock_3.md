**The strongest things in it:**

- **The record.** Every miss, revision, correction and error is in the ledger, including nine specification and code errors of Claude's.

**The honest deductions:**
- **Nothing was derived.** Every result is "consistent, not derived," and the input count never moved.
- **Two of the three 3D attempts went on Claude's bookkeeping,** not on ED.
- **Resolution was a hard limit.** Slices of 14,000 events are 24 across; many readings came back "too small to measure."
- **No number, nothing measurable.** Attempt 7 did not get closer to a prediction than attempt 6 was.
