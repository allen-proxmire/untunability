**The verdict labels used below:**

- **Consistent, not derived:** ED can say it without contradicting experiment, but the result was known or chosen knowing the answer.
- **A reason from ED's meanings:** it follows from what ED's words mean, and it isn't new physics.
- **Inherited:** taken from physics as is.

## 1. Gravity's source: the Jacobson route (road J, notes 2–5)

**Jacobson (1995) derived Einstein's equations from one physical assumption,** entropy proportional to horizon area, plus a temperature (Unruh's) and the first law of thermodynamics. Allen has long taken Jacobson as a guiding light. **So road J asked: can ED supply that assumption?**

**Step 1: the count grows like area** (note 3).
- **ED's entropy of a surface is the count of relations crossing it** (D6).
- **Relations that are finite, nearby, and point every way equally on average** cross a surface at a rate proportional to its area. A check confirmed it:
  - 1.011 against the formula for random segments;
  - 1.007 for a random short-range pattern;
  - with one pre-set expectation missed and explained openly (C7).

**Step 2: a finding about directions** (note 3).
- **On a regular grid, the count depends on which way a surface faces:** 1.00, 1.41, 1.73.
- **Jacobson needs the same answer for every direction.**
- **So ED's relations must point every way equally** (C9).

**Step 3: the key step, entropy is ED's reach, counted** (note 4).
- **Attempt 1 had already decided that "can't be brought back" means "out of reach of every future path",** a horizon, with a capacity of about 10¹²², the size of the cosmic horizon's entropy.
- **Entropy is missing information.** The present is all there is (D21).
- **So a horizon's entropy is the count of relations that belong to the present but reach beyond it.**
- **Allen confirmed the two steps as readings** of existing meanings (D7).

**Verdict** (C17): **ED's first input reduction.** Jacobson's area law is supplied by ED's own meanings, with nothing added and nothing fitted.
- **It is conditional** on the two readings, and on the pattern being three-dimensional and direction-free at horizon scales (assumed, not built).
- **G gets an ED meaning:** how densely relations cross a surface. Its value is still inherited (C10).

**Step 4: Unruh's temperature** (note 5).
- **A rigorous theorem ties it to boost symmetry:** space looking the same to every steady mover.
- **ED's finite-neighbour pattern has a rest frame,** so the temperature can only be **inherited,** with boost symmetry as a labelled assumption (C22).
- **Einstein's equation overall stays consistent, not derived. Road J closed** (RD7).

## 2. How waves travel: the dispersion check (note 6)

**The risk:** if short waves travel even slightly slower than light, atoms can feel large Lorentz violation at everyday energies (Husain and Louko). Discrete hopping often causes exactly that.

| rule, built from ED's meanings | result |
|---|---|
| **1D light** (no turning) | **Exactly light speed at every wavelength** |
| **1D matter** (turning) | A top speed below light: that's mass |
| **Simplest 3D light rule** | **Picks out directions,** and is **slower than light on average** |

**A general point:** any one-tick rule on a 3D grid *must* slow some short waves below light speed.

**Verdict** (C25): **no conflict,** because ED hasn't decided a 3D hop rule. **Any future rule must:**
- pick out no directions;
- not slow short waves below light;
- keep one locus per tick as the top speed.

## 3. The particle, repaired (road P, note 7)

**Attempt 4's conflict:** particles as uncuttable knots can't annihilate, but electrons and positrons do (PET scans detect it).

**The repair** (D10):
1. **"Commitments never stop" now means "what happened stays happened."** A commitment can end; its having happened stays reflected in the present.
2. **Commitments cut and rejoin only in pairs.** No strand just dangles.
3. **A particle's kind is kept through histories without handles.** This is a chosen rule, and it's labelled as a fitting risk.

**What it gives:**
- **A knot and its mirror image with the direction reversed can shrink to nothing together,** leaving light. That's annihilation, and pair creation runs backward.
- **Unlike kinds can't fully annihilate,** as in physics.
- **Attempt 4's three-dimensions result survives:** more than one lasting kind still exists only in three dimensions.

**Associations, logged as hints, not results:**
- **"Antiparticle = mirror image, running backward"** matches Feynman's picture of antiparticles as particles moving backward in time.
- **It also matches the old ED repo's "matter read against the grain of time".**

**Verdict** (C31): **particle meaning repaired, with a labelled rule; consistent, not derived.** Road P closed (RD11).

## 4. The grain (note 7)

**Allen's struggle** (D9): "EDs grid is supposed to be relational, scaleless, but in the committed world it relies on the Planck length... at some point, I think we end up on it."

**What attempt 5 found:**
- **Only counts are real.** A pattern of relations has no metres in it.
- **The Planck length is how densely relations cross a surface** (road J), not the size of a tile.
- **Discrete doesn't have to mean a grid.** Grids failed both the direction test and the slow-wave test, and a direction-free random pattern avoids both.

**Decided** (D10):
- **the grain is a count, not a length;**
- **ED's pattern is direction-free and random, not a grid.**

## 5. Why exactly one time dimension (road T, note 9)

**Physics assumes one time dimension. Road T asked whether ED supplies it.**

**The chain** (C34):
1. **Time is the order of relations** (A4 D11), and what happened stays happened (D10). So the order runs one way (T-Q1, a reading).
2. **Where things look smooth,** that order comes with an interval, the pairings count generalized, with the number of time directions left open (T-Q2, a labelled assumption).
3. **Future and past are separate only when there's exactly one time direction.**
   - **With two or more,** "later" can be turned smoothly into "earlier", and no one-way order is possible.
   - **With none,** there's no order at all.

**Verdict** (C37): **one time dimension, a reason from ED's meanings; a reduction by one input,** conditional on the reading and the assumption.
- **The interval's quadratic shape stays inherited,** as in physics.
- **The argument is known** (Tegmark, Dorling, causal sets), so it isn't new physics.
- **Road T closed** (RD15).
