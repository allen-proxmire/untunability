# Where ED stands: stock take

*Refreshed 2026-09-14 (replaces the 2026-09-13 version). Ledger at this point: 290 claims, 51 rule decisions (RD1–RD51), definitions D1–D18, 32 checks that rerun with one command. Details live in `01_Ledger`; the reasoning about what's solid and what's shaky is in [Audit.md](Audit.md) (section 7 is the latest).*

## In one paragraph

**ED has a definite, runnable commitment rule.** Patterns spread on a graph. Meetings leave marks in committed matter. A draw fixes only the record once the mark can't be brought back. A budget slows clocks and motion near mass, and loci keep being born.

**Where it has been tested, it reproduces known physics:**
- **Quantum:** interference, Englert's which-way trade-off, the quantum eraser, no faster-than-light signalling.
- **Gravity:** it is general relativity in budget language at every order checked (light bending, Mercury, Nordtvedt).
- **Dark energy:** a plain cosmological constant fits the distance data; ED's distinctive versions didn't.

**What's new since the last stock take.**
- **The handedness line:** ED's rule *can* settle into a handed state picked by chance, but only under three choices of ours.
- **The draw:** rebuilt so it is no longer loose about when, what and where.

**ED still has no new prediction that could be tested.** That is the honest bottom line.

## 2. What ED assumes now

| topic | decision |

Because the horizon is permanent, draws are exact only up to its capacity of about 10¹²² (RD31 revised). A draw fixes only the record, for the part that interacts (RD22). In simulations, redundancy (3 near-perfect copies) stands in for the horizon, as a modelling choice |

## 5. Where ED could still say something testable

1. **Handedness.** Only if ED fixes the standing phase and the committed-matter response from its own primitives. Then the size and pattern of a chosen hand would follow instead of being set. Not there yet.
2. **Exact draws up to 10¹²².** A real difference in principle from theories with no final draw, but not testable.
3. **Nothing in gravity or dark energy.** Both converged on standard physics.

**Bottom line.** The rule is honest, runnable, and consistent with everything checked. Its distinctive content is structure and interpretation: commitment, draws at the horizon, a hand chosen by the state. It is not yet a testable prediction.
