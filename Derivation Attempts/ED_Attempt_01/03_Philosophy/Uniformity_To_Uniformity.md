1. **The two uniformities aren't the same state.** With gravity, a smooth start is *low* entropy and a smooth end is *high* entropy (C260).

3. **It constrains the ends, not the middle.** As stated it predicts nothing yet beyond what ED already assumes. The special start is imposed, as in the Past Hypothesis.
