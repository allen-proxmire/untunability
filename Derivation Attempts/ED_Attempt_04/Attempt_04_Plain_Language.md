## How this attempt works

**Attempts 1–3 each ended under their own exit rules.** Attempt 3 slipped by treating a particle as one locus, because the picture was never written down. **So attempt 4 began by writing the picture down, before any test.**

**Three habits run through it:**
- **Allen decides what ED means.** When a meaning question comes up, Claude proposes the **least-structure answer** (the *default*) with the reason, and Allen accepts it, changes it, or vetoes it. Nothing is treated as decided until Allen says so.
- **Every line of work gets an exit rule before anything is computed,** and rules aren't revised after a run.
- **Allen's stance** (D12): "most of physics is known. ED doesn't argue, the experiments remain the same. But the issues still remain." So **"consistent, not derived"** is an expected, honest outcome. What ED is after are the open issues: why three dimensions, what makes gravity, why these numbers.

**The verdict labels used below:**
- **Closed:** a line ended under its exit rule.
- **Consistent, not derived:** ED can say it without contradicting experiment, but the result was known or chosen knowing the answer.
- **A reason from ED's meanings:** it follows from what ED's words mean, and it's still not new physics.

## 2. Gravity and motion

### What doesn't make gravity (roads R1 and R3) — closed

**The bar** (C3). General relativity needs a source to count its parts with these weights:

| part | needed | plain counting gives |
|---|---|---|
| Motion | 2 | ½ |
| Gravity's own energy | 2 | −½ |
| Internal energy | 1 | 1 |
| Pressure | 3 | 0 |

- **R1:** commitments can restate these weights (it's a known 1930s formula) but not derive them. They can't carry gravity's own energy (C5–C8).
- **R3:** attempt 3's clock result stands with presence and influence kept apart (C57).

### One rule for motion (road R4, notes 11–13)

**Allen's idea grew in three steps:**
1. **"Loci want to be on"** (D7);
2. **"clocks want to sync"** (D20);
3. **"put it all together"** (D25): **a body's path makes its own commitment count as large as it can.** Height raises the rate; speed lowers it.

- **At everyday speeds this is Newton's rule,** so throws, falls and orbits are one rule. Physics calls it the principle of maximal aging (C71, C72).
- **Sync with the ground** is just what's left when the ground stops the ball.
- **The old ED repo's local-sync result** was added by hand, and it refused global sync (C63, C64).

**The speed part** (notes 13; C74–C78). Allen: speed is "zipping through loci, committing at each"; ticking is "the other part", a duality.
- **Physics already draws it this way.** Feynman's checkerboard and Penrose's zigzag electron picture a massive particle as zigzagging at c, with **mass as the turning.** Light never turns, so it has no ticks of its own.
- **How the budget splits decides everything:**

| a body's own ticks count… | fast muons (measured: live 29.33× longer) |
|---|---|
| hops left over after net motion | about 1720× longer: **ruled out** |
| **pairings of right and left hops** (Allen's choice) | **29.33×: agrees** |

- **(own ticks)² + (net motion)² = (budget)²:** a right triangle, like E² = (pc)² + (mc²)².
- **Verdict: consistent, not derived.** The working count is special relativity's interval written as hops, and it was chosen knowing the answer. One dimension only.

**Light speed and black holes** (D26, C73): near c a body's own clock stops, and at a horizon a hovering clock stops as seen from far away. Mass itself doesn't grow; the energy needed does.

### Gravity's self-feedback (road R4 part 2, note 14)

- **Gravity tests use ten numbers.** Five measure whether action equals reaction (C79).
- **With light bending and Mercury right,** which ED has on record, **action = reaction gives the bar exactly:** 2, 2, 1, 3, and a Moon-test number of 0. An exact arithmetic check confirmed it (C80).
- **Allen's decision** (D30): influence is mutual, carries motion, and light sends it.
- **Verdict: consistent, not derived** (C83). It's general relativity's known structure.
- **Honest note:** Allen's sync idea gave the motion rule, not these weights.

### The preferred-frame hole (note 15) — open

- **A theorem** (C84): a discrete pattern can have only **two of three**: discrete, finite neighbours, no rest frame.
- **ED as decided keeps the first two,** so its substrate has a rest frame (C85).
- **Hiding a frame at the smallest scale usually leaks into everyday physics** at about the percent level, unless something is finely tuned. **ED names nothing that stops that.**
- **The bounds are tight:**
  - light's speed doesn't depend on its energy up to about 6 Planck energies;
  - light shows no birefringence;
  - gravity's frame numbers are below about 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻⁹.
- **Decided** (D32): **ED keeps finite neighbours, and has no infinities.**
  - So its substrate has a rest frame, and **the hole is recorded open** against the bounds, with the fine-tuning leak as the obstacle (C87).
  - PF-Q2 is still open.
  - **The fork** ([Fork_PFd_Causal_Set](Fork_PFd_Causal_Set/)) looked at the other choice, giving up finite neighbours. It fits the time and motion side and costs the space side, and it's closed as a comparison.
- **Allen's follow-up:** no locus can have more neighbours than there are loci. That changes the wording, not the choice. With no infinities, exact frame-freedom was never possible, so the real question is how much frame shows at testable energies (C88).

## 3. Matter

- **As a place rule it conflicts with observation.** Bosons share place and state.
- **As sameness** (D9) it's Pauli exclusion. That needs three dimensions and spin, and ED has neither on its own.
- **Verdict: consistent, not derived.**

**Particles as knots (notes 7–9):**
- **The old ED repo's picture:** "a knot in a web, tethered". Tethered objects can indeed be fermions (Finkelstein and Rubinstein).
- **History:** Kelvin's knotted atoms failed because the knots weren't stable. Modern knotted field solutions are stable. Knots made in water untie by cutting.
- **Allen's decision:** a particle is a knot of commitments that can't be cut (D14, D16).
- **Open pokes:**
  - **annihilation:** a knot and its mirror image can't untie each other without cutting (C55);
  - **spin and charge** from knots.

## 4. Why three dimensions

**Verdict: a reason from ED's meanings, consistent, not new physics.**

**E-C as a shape** (note 12, C69, C70):
- **The quantity, named first:** how many kinds of loop the present can carry. Dimensions 1, 2, 3 and 4+ give 0, 1, many (as many as the loci allow, since ED has no infinities; C88), and 1.
- **Asking for more than one leaves the single point 3,** with zero free parameters.
- **"4 is never considered", exactly:** a four-dimensional region keeps its commitments, but no kind in it can be told apart.
- **Verdict:** a reason from ED's meanings, consistent, not new physics.
- **Holes:**
  - the small-scale dimension may be about 2, right where knots live;
  - it selects, it doesn't grow;
  - annihilation;
  - one dimension everywhere.

**Allen's cones and rotations** ([Cone_Sketch.html](Cone_Sketch.html), C50–C53):
- **Round cones** fill space only in two dimensions.
- **In Allen's corner,** pyramids fill exactly with 45° walls, but in every dimension.
- **Allen: "rotation does not."** d dimensions have d(d−1)/2 ways to rotate, which matches the number of axes **only in three.**

**Two roots** (C55). The three-dimensional facts come from two separate reasons, and each lands on exactly three:
- **A line and a plane just fit:** knots, rotations, string meetings.
- **How influence thins out:** stable orbits, gravity settling.

**What's already published** (C54):

- **Knots selecting three dimensions:** "Knotty inflation", 2017.
- **String gas cosmology.**
- **ED's own differences:** its knots are permanent by meaning, and a fourth direction needn't be hidden, because it never leaves anything the present keeps.

## 7. Annihilation, and the conclusion (note 17)

**Allen chose to test annihilation on paper, then conclude** (D34).

**Two conflicts** (C91):
- **Kind.** Reshaping without cutting keeps a knot's type, and knots have no inverses: no knot joined to another ever gives a plain loop. So a knotted particle can never annihilate.
- **Commitments.** Even an unknotted particle can't vanish, because commitments never stop. And turning into light would be uncommitting.

**What physics does** (C90):
- **Solitons annihilate** because their charge is an integer, and +1 and −1 cancel.
- **Knots can be undone in four-dimensional spacetime** only through saddle moves, which cut and rejoin.
- **So lasting kinds that can annihilate need a charge with inverses.**

**Verdict** (C92): **conflict recorded.** D14, D16 and "commitments never stop" go into attempt 5 as needing revision. Three leads come with them:
- **L1:** allow cutting and rejoining in pairs; E-C may survive.
- **L2:** use field charges; this weakens the reason for three.
- **L3:** read "never stop" as "what happened stays happened" (close to D21).
