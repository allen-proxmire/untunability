# Why things lean, and the synced ball: Allen's two thoughts

*ED_Attempt_04, note 4. 2026-09-15 (D7, D8). Ledger: C9, C12, C13, C14. Ideas recorded with what fits and what pokes back; nothing tested.*

## Thought 1: why do clocks lean toward slow time? (D7)

> "why do clocks 'want' to run slower? why do they lean toward slow time/rapid commitment. same with relativity: why do things roll down the fabric? there is no down. that sort of implies that loci want to be on (if others nearby are on) - they are pulling in stuff to blink more. its the draw toward uniformity that everything is going back to. that just moves the question, but its seems logical."

**The idea:** loci near on-loci tend to be on. Gravity is the pull toward more commitment. Things "fall" toward where commitment is denser, and clocks slow there.

**What fits (C13):**
- **It's in attempt 1's ideas already, in outline:** "a commitment influences its neighbours. Dense things pulse, in a kind of sync" (A1 D4).
- **It has the right sign.** Every mechanism so far only *subtracted*: exclusion, slowed spread, slowed sources. General relativity's self-feedback *adds* (A3 C43; A4 C7). "Loci want to be on near on-loci" is **cooperative**, so it adds. **It's the first idea on the table with the sign general relativity needs.**
- **It matches how falling works in the on/off picture.** Motion bends toward regions where loci are more often on, because motion slows there (A3 C38). "Down" is "toward more on-ness".

**What pokes back:**
- **When it arrived.** It came right after note 7 said adding was what's missing. So any rule built on it has to be fixed from its meaning *before* computing weights, or it's fitting.
- **It blurs D2.** If influence makes loci "want to be on", influence starts switching on presence. D2 says they're separate. Either "on" here means influence only, or D2 needs revisiting (Allen's call).
- **"The question moves",** as Allen says: *why* do loci want to be on? Thought 2 is his answer.

## Thought 2: the synced ball (D8)

> "remember i said the big bang ball was synced? i picture a pulsating ball of commitment. the center commits and it ripples out, updating across the ball. i know that gives an ununiformity of sorts. thats why i always add: relation among one kind of thing is weak, relations among different things is richer. theres some split there between this sync of commitment and the world that happens after the big bang ball sees a difference. the syncing gets disrupted, and the universe is trying to get back to that."

**The idea:**
- **The start:** the universe began as a synced, pulsing ball of commitment, updating in ripples from the centre.
- **The break:** once the ball "sees a difference", the sync is disrupted.
- **The drive:** everything since is the universe trying to get back to sync.
- **Relations:** among one kind of thing they're weak; among different things they're rich.

**Where it's already recorded:** attempt 1 logged the same picture as D16, "a uniform, synced ball of loci and commitments … heading back to uniformity", with "one is none" and Contrast First Ontology (A1 D16, C261, C266).

**What fits (C14):**
- **It gives D7 a reason.** Loci want to be on together because the world is pulling back toward sync.
- **"One kind weak, different kinds rich"** echoes how physics treats exclusion. Identical fermions exclude each other; different particles mix freely (R2, note 3). That's an association, not a finding.

**What pokes back (C9, C12, and attempt 1's record):**
- **Gravity runs the other way on uniformity.** With gravity, a smooth universe is *low* entropy and clumping *raises* it. That's why the early universe's smoothness is considered special (Penrose's Weyl curvature hypothesis; A1 C260, C265; C9). So "back to uniformity" can't mean back to the starting state. Attempt 1 already recorded this tension (A1 C261).
- **Gravity also pulls clocks *out* of sync.** Clocks deep in a gravity well run slower than those outside. Clumping makes clock rates *more* different. If "sync" means clocks agreeing everywhere, gravity works against it. It might mean something more local, like neighbours matching. That isn't pinned down yet.
- **The universe shows no centre.** The cosmic microwave background is the same in every direction to about 1 part in 100,000, and standard cosmology has no centre (C12). Ripples spreading from one centre would show up as a direction pattern, unless we happen to sit near the centre. A reading that avoids this: **every locus is its own centre**, and "the ripple" is sync spreading locally everywhere at once. That's untested.
- **Commitments are permanent in ED,** so "getting back" can only mean looking uniform again, not undoing history (A1 C261).
