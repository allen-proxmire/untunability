# The picture (attempt 4, draft for Allen)

*ED_Attempt_04, note 1. 2026-09-15 (RD1). Ledger: C1, C2. A paper note; nothing is tested here. **Everything marked "proposed" is a default for Allen to accept, change or veto.***

## Why start here

Attempt 3 slipped by treating a particle as one locus (A3 C45). That happened because the picture was never written down, and "on" quietly meant two different things. **This note pins the words down first**, so every later road answers to the same picture.

## What ED means so far (the philosophy, in one place)

These are the meanings decided or found across attempts 1–3, in plain words:
- **Things become definite by committing.** A draw fixes only the record of which way things went. It happens when that record is out of reach (A1 RD28, RD50).
- **The chances aren't a choice.** Given ED's structure and no faster-than-light signalling, probability is amplitude times its conjugate (A2 C11).
- **Space is discrete and grows.** Loci come into being and persist (A1 RD20, RD21). Only counts are real, not sizes in metres (A1 RD15).
- **A locus can be full** (A3 D2).
- **Loci are on or off. A body's loci are on, committing to other loci of the same body** (A3 D4).
- **A particle is many loci** (A3 D5).

## Open meaning decisions (Allen decides)

| | question | proposed default (least structure) | why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | Does a body's own presence block its own processes? | **No.** Presence excludes *other* bodies, not the body itself | Otherwise every body's clocks stop inside it |
| **Q2** | Is gravity's influence a kind of "on", or a separate quantity? | **Separate:** a chance of blocking, never presence itself | Keeps the two meanings apart; the e^(−Φ) result depends on it (A3 C38, C45) |
| **Q3** | What sets a particle's mass: how many loci it has, or what it sends out? | **What it sends out** (its total influence), not its locus count | Otherwise heavier particles would have to be bigger |
| **Q4** | What does a commitment carry? | **Nothing numerical yet:** just "these two loci belong to one body", until a rule is written | Road 1 asks whether commitments add to gravity; the guard is fixing this before computing |
| **Q5** | Can two bodies be present at the same locus? | **No** (a full locus) | Gives "matter excludes matter". It must then face the known physics of exclusion |
| **Q6** | Do attempt 1's smooth budget and its cap tied to gravity still apply? | **Retired,** replaced by presence plus influence | Attempt 3's black-hole conflict came from that cap (A3 C33) |
