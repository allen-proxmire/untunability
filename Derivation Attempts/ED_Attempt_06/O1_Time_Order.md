# Time order as the missing ingredient (on paper)

*ED_Attempt_06, note 15. 2026-09-16 (RD27). Ledger: C82–C85. Literature and reasoning; nothing computed. **Meaning questions O-Q1–O-Q3 and the draft exit rule are for Allen.** The strong-sync model runs continue meanwhile (D20).*

## The question

**Both growth models built a *spatial* pattern:** undirected links, rewired freely.

**ED's firmest meaning is that time is a one-way order** (A5 D11), and neither model used it.

**Could time order be what makes a pattern smooth?**

## What's known (C82)

| | what it says | source |
|---|---|---|

| **Causality is the key ingredient** | CDT keeps only geometries where **space doesn't change topology as time goes on**: no branching, no merging. That is what lets a smooth four-dimensional world appear | CDT reviews (arXiv:1004.0352, 1111.6938) |

| **Branchy geometries** | Branched polymers have spectral dimension 4/3 | Branched-polymer literature |

## On paper

### 1. The models grew space without time (C83)

- **In both models, links had no direction.** A rewire could join any two nearby points, including joining an event back into its own past.
- **In causal terms, that allows space to branch and merge freely.** That's the freedom CDT's founders found produces fractal and crumpled geometries.
- **What the models produced fits that family:**
  - rings, where the tight floor forbids branching entirely but also forbids everything else;
  - rough tangles (walk dimension about 2.6, return dimension about 1, fractal-like);
  - small worlds (crumpled, no finite dimension).
- **This match is suggestive, not proof.** The numbers aren't the branched-polymer values, and the models aren't CDT's.

### 2. ED already has "order plus number" (C83)

**Two decided meanings line up with Sorkin's slogan:**
- **order:** time is a strict one-way order (A5 D11);
- **number:** the grain is a count, not a length (A5 D10).

**By the HKMM theorem,** order plus count carries the geometry.

**So in ED, the pattern shouldn't be grown as a spatial graph at all.**
- **It should be the order itself:** relations point from earlier to later.
- **Space is a slice:** a set of events none of which comes before another.

That's a reframing of what "growing the pattern" means.

### 3. It inherits two known problems (C84)

- **Neighbours:** a causal set is only Lorentz invariant with infinitely many nearest neighbours (A4 C84). **ED chose finite neighbours** (D32). That puts ED nearer CDT's side: finite pieces and a rest frame.
- **Dynamics:** most orders aren't space-like, and no known growth rule picks the space-like ones. Causal set theory hasn't solved this.

### 4. What CDT's lesson says for ED (C84)

- **Its lesson is specific:** don't let space branch or merge as time goes on.
- **In ED terms,** a slice of space should stay in one piece from one step to the next.
- **ED's meanings may support this.** "What happened stays happened" and paired cut-and-rejoin (A5 D10) restrict how relations can change. But that a slice can't split is a reading taken *from what's known to work*, so it carries fitting risk and is labelled as such.
- **The bar still applies** (A4 C59): CDT puts the dimension in through its four-dimensional building blocks. ED has no building blocks.
  - So causality addresses **roughness** (wall 3, smoothness).
  - The **dimension** would still come from the sync floor plus commitment's push (C33).

## What it means (C84)

- **The smoothness wall now points at a specific omission:** the models never used ED's time order.
- **In the literature, causality is exactly what separates smooth large-scale geometry from branchy, fractal and crumpled geometry.**
- **ED already contains "order plus number" and finite neighbours.** So the growth question becomes a known open problem in a specific form: **a growth rule for a finite-neighbour order that doesn't let space branch, and gives a space-like pattern.**
- **For tonight's model results:** M2 lacks this ingredient, so its failure to reach three is expected and isn't decisive against ED. It's a test of space-without-time.
- **Verdict shape:** consistent, not derived. Inputs are unchanged (still 3).
