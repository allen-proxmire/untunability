1. **Nothing is left for ED to conserve.** Both totals are already held at flat, including the average curvature — which CDT has to tune. *What CDT tunes, ED conserves* now covers both of a slice's settings. (C2)
2. **The rival theory's extended space comes from counting, with a causal step between slices** — not from cancellation, and not only from a tuned strength. (C3)
3. **Counting gives exactly the kind of push attempt 9 wanted** — strong, growing with size, no tuned strength. **But which way it pushes depends entirely on ED's moves.** (C9, C14, C20)

5. **The earlier push toward flat came from inconsistent moves**, which counting can't use. (C17)
