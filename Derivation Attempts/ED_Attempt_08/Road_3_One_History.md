# Road 3, on paper: can one grown history give smooth space?

*ED_Attempt_08, note 9. 2026-09-20 (RD15). Ledger: C39–C41. A conversation with Allen, recorded. **Reasoning only; nothing computed.** Written plainly.*

## The question road E ended on

**Every approach that gets smooth spacetime does it by adding up many possible shapes and letting the wild ones cancel.** ED grows one pattern, tick by tick. Is that a genuine tension inside ED's own meanings?

## What "a sum over histories" means, plainly

A particle goes from A to B. The commonsense question is *which path?* Quantum mechanics says don't ask: let **every** path contribute, each with a weight, and add them up.

**The weights can cancel.** Wild paths point in all directions and wipe each other out; near the sensible path, neighbours agree and reinforce.

> **The smooth answer is not one of the paths being smooth. It is what survives after the wild ones destroy each other.**

Do the same for space: take every shape spacetime could be, weight them, add. At large scales the result looks smooth — **though no single shape in the sum is.**

## Where the theories actually stand (C39)

**Allen asked whether other theories say there is not one history. They disagree, and most do not need to commit.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The sum itself** | A calculating device. You can use it and still hold that exactly one thing happened |
| **Many-worlds** | All branches real |
| **Collapse readings** | One happens; the sum is bookkeeping |
| **Consistent histories** (Gell-Mann, Hartle, Griffiths) | A framework about *sets* of histories and when probabilities may be assigned to them — **the closest to ED's picture** |
| **CDT** | Uses the sum to compute geometry; takes no position on what "happened" |

**Allen's "it seems like a math trick" is half right.** It is a calculational device in most readings — one that keeps being correct, which usually means the world does something structurally like it.

## Allen's decision (C39)

> **"Once something has happened it's fixed, but many could have happened."**

**So "what happened stays happened" does not bar ED from being an ensemble theory.** Roads 1 and 2 are not ruled out on principle, and neither is a sum.

## Allen's clarification, which corrects the record (C40)

> **"A node doesn't stay committed, it passes it on. The history isn't recorded anywhere or known to the present. To the present, any set of events that led to the present is possible. You can undo history, but you can't really know what it was."**

**This changes how ED has been described in Claude's notes.** If the present does not encode a unique past, **ED already holds that the past is a set of possibilities rather than a recorded thread.** ED is an ensemble theory and has been all along; the growth model simply never treated it as one.

### Two corrections Claude owed, recorded

**1. "Stringy" gave the wrong picture.** A slice reading 1.4 describes **the shape of space at one tick** — a tangle of thin filaments filling a region, like a web. **Nothing persists.** It is not strands of existence running through time; the pattern is remade every tick. Allen read it as worldlines, reasonably, because of how Claude wrote it.

**2. Forbidding, catching and cancelling are three different things,** and Claude's wording let them blur:

| | what it does |
|---|---|
| **Forbidding** (the budget, the ceiling) | Removes shapes from the pool. Blunt; it works; it is why ED cannot crumple |
| **Catching** (A7 C3b) | Each meaning **flags** its own bad shape. Detection, after the fact |
| **Cancelling** | Lets the shapes in and has them **destroy each other**. **ED does not do this** |

**Only the third produces smoothness.**

## The gap, stated as plainly as it can be (C41)

**Being an ensemble does not rescue the growth rule, and the reason is sharp.**

**ED's ensemble is classical: every weight is positive.** A classical ensemble is dominated by whatever is most **numerous** — and stringy shapes are overwhelmingly more numerous than smooth ones, the way a shuffled deck is never in order. No amount of local preference outvotes that count.

> **Cancellation is what beats "more numerous". Adding positive numbers can never cancel. You need weights that can point in opposite directions.**

**And ED already has them.** The Born rule is one of ED's three supplied inputs — that is the machinery of amplitudes. **The growth model uses none of it.** It assumes the Born rule at the foundations and then grows the universe with dice.

**Allen reached for this unprompted** ("I somehow thought the wobble or ED's Born rule psi\* played a role somewhere — spitballing cancellation ideas"). That instinct points at the right place.

**A precise version exists and is not exotic: a quantum walk in place of a classical random walk.** Same structure, amplitudes instead of probabilities, interference instead of averaging. ED already uses random walks throughout its readings.

## Allen's Deutsch analogy, recorded because it holds

> *"If you recall the quantum computing, Deutsch's oracle — the math function being queried is the pattern in the substrate. What is a function? Sum/histories, I'm guessing."*

**In Deutsch's problem you learn a global property of a function in one query, by asking in superposition and letting interference do the work. Checking inputs one at a time cannot do it.**

**That is the exact shape of road E's failure.** Smoothness is a global property of a slice; the growth model checks one move at a time. The analogy is apt, not loose.

## What is decided and what is not

**Decided (D11, D12):** the past is fixed once it happens but many pasts were possible; the present does not know its own past; a node passes its commitment on rather than keeping it.

**Not decided, and not proposed as a decision:** whether ED's growth should carry **amplitudes** rather than probabilities. That is a large change, it touches the Born rule input directly, and it is Allen's alone.

**What it would open if taken:** a fourth road — growth with interference — in which the wild shapes cancel rather than being forbidden, and in which ED's own Born rule input would finally be doing work in the growth model rather than sitting above it.
